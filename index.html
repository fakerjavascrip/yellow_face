CHARACTER PROFILE / 人物档案

典韦 · Dian Wei

忠勇果敢 骁勇善战 黄色脸谱

典韦是东汉末年曹操麾下的著名猛将， 以过人的力量、勇猛的战斗能力和 忠诚无畏的精神而闻名。

Dian Wei was a renowned warrior serving under Cao Cao during the late Eastern Han Dynasty. He was celebrated for his extraordinary strength, exceptional fighting skills, and unwavering loyalty.

在传统戏曲艺术中，典韦常以鲜明的 黄色脸谱登场，通过夸张的眉眼、 强烈的色彩和威武的身段， 展现其勇猛刚烈的人物性格。

In traditional Chinese opera, Dian Wei is often represented by a striking yellow facial mask. Exaggerated facial patterns, bold colors, and powerful movements highlight his fierce and courageous personality.

01 / CHARACTER STORY

壹 · 人物故事

典韦以忠诚和勇武著称，是曹操麾下的重要护卫。 建安二年（197年），在宛城之战中， 他奋力抵御敌军，最终战死。

Dian Wei was renowned for his loyalty and bravery as one of Cao Cao's trusted guards. In 197 CE, during the Battle of Wancheng, he fought fiercely against enemy forces and ultimately lost his life.

他的故事象征着忠诚、责任与无畏， 并成为传统文学和戏曲艺术中 富有代表性的英雄叙事。

His story symbolizes loyalty, duty, and fearlessness, making him a memorable heroic figure in traditional literature and Chinese opera.

02 / FACIAL MASK CULTURE

贰 · 黄色脸谱文化

在传统戏曲脸谱的色彩表现中， 黄色常用于突出勇猛、刚烈和骁悍的 人物特征。典韦的黄色脸谱 具有鲜明的视觉识别性。

In traditional opera mask symbolism, yellow often conveys courage, fierceness, and a powerful temperament. Dian Wei's yellow mask gives him a distinctive visual identity.

夸张的眉形、锐利的眼部轮廓和 对称的面部纹样，共同塑造出 威严而富有力量感的英雄形象。

Exaggerated eyebrows, sharp eye contours, and symmetrical facial patterns work together to create an imposing and powerful heroic image.

03 / HEBEI BANGZI OPERA

叁 · 河北梆子艺术

河北梆子是中国传统地方戏曲剧种， 以高亢激越的唱腔、鲜明的节奏和 富有感染力的舞台表演而著称。

Hebei Bangzi is a traditional regional Chinese opera known for its powerful vocal style, distinctive rhythms, and expressive stage performances.

本作品将戏曲人物、传统脸谱与 NFC 数字音频技术相结合， 让观众通过实体海报进入数字空间， 进一步了解人物故事与戏曲文化。

This project combines traditional opera characters, facial mask design, and NFC audio technology. It connects physical posters with digital experiences, allowing audiences to explore character stories and traditional opera culture.
